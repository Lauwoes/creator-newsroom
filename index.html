Creator deskRun like a newsroom, not a campaign · a simulation on real data

Day 01
Built for the NYU SPS × Google Marketing Accelerator Hackathon, Track 3 (Process and Go-To-Market Strategy), as the functional prototype behind the Creator Newsroom submission. A student prototype, not a Google product; Google product names describe what the pilot desk would use. Brooks Running is a real brand chosen for the simulation and is not involved. The bench is sixteen real public YouTube channels, found by public search; none was contacted and nothing on this site is attributed to them. Trend signals are real and link to their sources; assignments, briefs, scripts and outcomes are simulated; rates and momentum are modelled, formulas shown. What is real, what is modelled, what is simulated · Source and data on GitHub. Progress is kept in this browser only.
