Set it up · about 20 minutes

Before you start. Use a computer, not a phone. Use a personal Google account, not a work or school one, in a browser window signed in to only that account. Anyone who has your desk link can read your rules, so type nothing secret.

  1. Get a Gemini key. Go to Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com), choose "Get API key", then "Create API key". Add prepaid credit when it asks; a few dollars is plenty. Never paste the key into an email, a chat or this page.
  2. Copy the desk's code. Press "Copy the code" below.
    Read the code on GitHub
  3. Open script.new in a new tab. Click "Untitled project" at the top and name it, for example "Creator desk". Click in the code area, press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on a Mac), paste, then press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S).
  4. Add your key. Click the gear on the left ("Project Settings"). Under "Script Properties" press "Add script property". Property: GEMINI_API_KEY. Value: your key. Press "Save script properties".
  5. Run setup. Click "< >" on the left to go back to the code. In the bar above the code, check the menu shows "setup", then press "Run".
  6. Say yes to Google. Google will say it has not verified this app. That is expected: this copy is yours, and Google has not reviewed it. Click "Advanced", then "Go to (your project name)". Tick every box and continue. One box lets the script edit your Google Sheets; it only opens the one Sheet it creates.
  7. Wait for "Setup worked" in the log under the code. The line above it is the link to your new Sheet. Open it and bookmark it. Running setup again never erases your work. If a red error appears instead, read it: it says what to fix. Then run setup again.
  8. Put it online. Top right: "Deploy", then "New deployment". Click the gear beside "Select type" and choose "Web app". Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Not "Anyone with a Google account". Not "Only myself". Press "Deploy" and copy the "Web app URL". It ends in /exec.
  9. Open your desk. Paste that address in the box at the top of this page and press "Open my desk". You should see the starter rules. Bookmark the page: that is your desk link. The page changes to your desk. To come back to these steps, press Start your own desk at the bottom.
  10. Make it yours. In your Sheet, fill in the Brand tab, edit the Standards tab, then add your creators to the Bench tab. Press Enter after each change, then reload your desk page. The page tells you if it could not read a row.

Afterwards. Share the desk link with creators. Share the Sheet only with your own team, and never share the script. To pause the desk, set "Desk is" to Off in the Brand tab. The "Read me" tab in your Sheet says how to take it down.

(Button and menu names are Google's and may read slightly differently; pick the closest match.)

What this is not

  • YouTube channels only. English only.
  • The starter headings follow United States advertising rules. They are not legal advice.
  • Two parts of the example: standards and bench. No trend board, no assignments, no dashboard.
  • No sign-in, no approvals, no record of who decided what. Anyone who can edit the Sheet can change the rules.
  • Anyone with a desk link can read that desk's standards, and its bench if the owner switched it on.
  • Gemini can be wrong both ways: it can clear a draft it should flag, and flag one that is fine. How often has not been measured.
  • The code does not update itself. Each desk runs the copy its owner pasted.
  • Each desk lives in one person's Google account and runs on that person's Gemini key.
